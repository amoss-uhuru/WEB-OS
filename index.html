How the platform works

One account. One place for every service.

Built so a resident with a basic smartphone and a slow connection can still get things done in under a minute.

1

Browse without an account

Projects, announcements, resources and opportunities are open to everyone.

2

Register once

Create an account with your name, phone number and ward to unlock feedback and applications.

3

Get verified

Confirm your phone via OTP and your ward/village so updates reach the right people.

4

Participate

Submit feedback, apply for bursaries and opportunities, and track everything from your dashboard.

Have an issue to report?

Every submission gets a tracking reference — from first report to resolution.
